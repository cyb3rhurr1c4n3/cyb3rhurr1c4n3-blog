I work in offensive security - operating in red team engagements, building the tools that run them, and researching the mechanics that make the difference between a technique that works and one that gets caught.

The part I find most interesting isn't the exploit itself - it's the layer underneath: why a behavioral chain triggers a detection rule, what the telemetry looks like from the other side, and how that understanding changes the way you build and operate. That's the thread connecting everything I do: execution, development, and research - each one making the other more effective.

This blog is where I document what I'm building, what I'm figuring out, and occasionally what broke and why that turned out to be the more interesting problem.
